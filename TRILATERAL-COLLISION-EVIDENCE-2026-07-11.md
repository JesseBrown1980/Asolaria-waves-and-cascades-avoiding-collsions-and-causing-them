## Keep four evidence layers separate

### Deterministic source behavior

PID hashes, category precedence, room rotation, route planning, and other named source mechanisms are
`MEASURED_REPO` when the code/tests exist and seat-specific `MEASURED` when rerun.

### Mathematical principles

CRT/Bézout, ring isomorphism, birthday bounds, and unitary/Parseval identities are mathematical
statements with declared domains. They are not runtime throughput measurements.

### Asolaria architecture/canon

Execution-lane isolation, search-lane collision, cascade-wave interpretation, and the wider translator
ladder are tagged per the owning canon and measured cells. Canon is real architecture, but each
specific live or exact rung still needs its own evidence.

### Physical analogy

Frequency combs and wave interference can ground an analogy or inspire a carrier. They do not prove
that a classical software collision surface is a physical optical or quantum experiment.

## Inflation errors

Reject:

- distinct prime residues said to “never alias” without stating the shared modulus/range/domain;
- address collision resistance presented as mathematical impossibility for truncated hashes;
- a GNN-selected interference peak presented as automatically byte-exact source reconstruction;
- software waves presented as literal optical/quantum waves;
- operator throughput targets presented as measured completed work.
